隐私政策

易识 / WrenRec · 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 9 日 · 最近更新：2026 年 11 月 8 日

易识（英文名 WrenRec）是一款录音转文字应用，语音识别完全在你的设备本机进行。本政策说明我们处理哪些数据、如何处理以及数据何时会离开设备。简而言之：你的录音和文字只保存在你的设备上，我们不收集、不上传、不追踪任何数据，也不使用任何第三方云端 AI 服务。

一、我们处理的数据及其位置

录音、导入文件与转写文本：你录制的音频、通过系统选择器主动导入的音视频文件及其转写结果，均只存储在你的设备本地（App 沙盒及 App Group 容器内）。这些内容仅用于生成、展示、编辑和由你主动导出转写结果。语音识别全部在本机由离线 AI 模型完成，不经过任何服务器或第三方 AI 服务。

诊断与崩溃日志：日志仅保存在本机，可能包含错误信息、文件路径及识别进度，用于排查运行问题。只有当你主动通过系统分享面板或邮件发送日志时，日志才会离开设备。反馈邮件还可能包含 App 版本、设备型号和系统版本；发送前你可以查看、修改或取消邮件。

二、我们不收集的数据

本应用没有账号系统，不包含任何统计、分析或广告 SDK，不收集设备标识符，不进行用户追踪。我们不会自动向任何第三方传输或共享你的录音、导入文件、转写文本或日志，也没有服务器接收你的内容。

三、网络用途与离线识别

识别功能的网络访问仅用于下载语音识别模型。首次使用某个模型时，App 会通过 Apple-hosted Background Assets（苹果托管的后台资源服务）将模型文件下载到你的设备。下载内容仅为模型文件，请求不携带你的录音、转写文本、个人数据或用户标识符。

所选模型下载完成后，识别不再需要网络。你可以开启飞行模式并关闭 Wi-Fi，然后继续录音或导入音频并完成转写。只有下载其他尚未保存到本机的模型时，才需要再次联网。

四、系统权限说明

麦克风：用于录制语音并转写为文字，仅在你主动开始录音时使用。

后台音频：用于录音过程中锁屏或切换应用时继续采集，录音停止后立即释放。

文件与照片：仅处理你通过系统文件或照片选择器主动选择的内容，不会自行读取或上传你的其他文件。

五、导出与分享

你可以通过系统分享面板主动导出录音、转写文本或字幕，也可以主动分享诊断日志。只有在你确认导出、分享或发送后，所选内容才会由你选择的应用或服务接收。本应用不会自动执行这些操作，也不会控制接收方随后的数据处理；请在分享前确认接收方及分享内容。

六、数据的删除

你可以在 App 内随时删除任意录音记录及其转写文本。删除 App 会同时删除其全部本地数据。删除操作不可恢复，请通过「导出全部记录」功能定期备份重要内容。你已经主动导出或分享的文件需要由你在相应位置单独删除。

七、儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意识地收集儿童信息。由于我们不收集或上传用户数据，不存在儿童数据被本应用上传的风险。

八、政策变更

如本政策发生变更，我们会更新本页面并标注更新日期。重大变更时，会在 App 内以适当方式提示。

九、联系我们

如对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过 支持与联系 页面中的邮箱与我们联系。

Privacy Policy (English)

WrenRec · Effective date: October 9, 2026 · Last updated: November 8, 2026

WrenRec is a voice-to-text app that performs all speech recognition entirely on your device. This policy explains what data is processed, how it is used, and when it may leave your device. In short: your recordings and transcripts remain on your device. We do not collect, upload, or track your data, and we do not use any third-party cloud AI service.

1. Data We Process and Where It Is Stored

Recordings, imported files, and transcripts: Audio you record, media files you explicitly import through the system picker, and their transcripts are stored only on your device in the app sandbox and App Group container. They are used only to generate, display, edit, and export transcripts at your request. Speech recognition is performed locally by on-device AI models and does not pass through any server or third-party AI service.

Diagnostic and crash logs: Logs remain on your device and may contain error messages, file paths, and transcription progress to help diagnose technical problems. They leave your device only if you explicitly share them through the system share sheet or email them to us. A feedback email may also include the app version, device model, and operating-system version. You can review, edit, or cancel the email before sending it.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

WrenRec has no account system and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not collect device identifiers or track users. We do not automatically transmit or share recordings, imported files, transcripts, or logs with any third party, and we operate no server that receives your content.

3. Network Use and Offline Transcription

Network access for the recognition feature is used only to download speech-recognition models. When a model is used for the first time, the app downloads its files to your device through Apple-hosted Background Assets. These downloads contain model files only; the requests do not include your recordings, transcripts, personal data, or user identifiers.

Once the selected model has been downloaded, transcription does not require a network connection. You can enable Airplane Mode and disable Wi-Fi, then record or import audio and complete transcription normally. A connection is needed again only to download another model that is not already stored on your device.

4. System Permissions

Microphone: Used to record speech for transcription only when you explicitly start recording.

Background audio: Used to continue recording when you lock the screen or switch apps, and released when recording stops.

Files and Photos: The app processes only items you explicitly select through the system file or photo picker. It does not independently read or upload your other files.

5. Exporting and Sharing

You may explicitly export recordings, transcripts, or subtitles through the system share sheet, or share diagnostic logs. Selected content is received by the app or service you choose only after you confirm the export, share, or message. WrenRec does not perform these actions automatically and does not control the recipient’s subsequent data handling. Review the recipient and selected content before sharing.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete any recording and its transcript within the app. Deleting the app also deletes its local data. Deletion cannot be undone, so use “Export All Records” to back up important content regularly. Files you have explicitly exported or shared must be deleted separately in their respective locations.

7. Children’s Privacy

WrenRec is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children’s information. Because the app does not collect or upload user data, it does not upload children’s data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We will update this page and its revision date when this policy changes. Material changes will also be communicated in the app where appropriate.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us using the email address on the Support page.
